This year has witnessed a remarkable surge in the capabilities and real-world adoption of AI agents, with coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex demonstrating impressive capabilities in real-world applications.
However, how to train the models that power these agents through reinforcement learning remains an open challenge.

Motivated by this, Uni-Agent is designed as a unified framework that connects diverse agent harnesses and tasks to reinforcement learning pipelines for training long-horizon LLM agents.
